About

Kevin Radtke is a contemporary artist based in Portland, Oregon, working across painting, digital media, and sculpture. His work investigates the interplay between natural forms and digital abstraction.

With a background in fine arts and a deep curiosity for emerging technologies, Kevin creates pieces that blur the boundary between the handmade and the algorithmic. His work has been exhibited at galleries in New York, Los Angeles, Berlin, and Tokyo.

When not in the studio, he teaches workshops on creative coding and experimental art techniques.
